### **1. Open Terminal**
```bash
cd c:\Users\sqavi\Nexora-ai
streamlit run app.py
```

### **2. Open Browser**
Visit: **http://localhost:8502**

### **3. Try It**
- Click: **⚙️ AI Workflow Generator** (left sidebar)
- Copy this: `Send email to syeduddin827@gmail.com that tomorrow is holiday`
- Paste into the text area
- Click: **🚀 Generate Workflow**
- Click: **💾 SAVE WORKFLOW**
- Click: **🎮 Controls** in sidebar
- Select your workflow
- Click: **Execute Workflow**
- Check your email inbox ✅

**Done!** You just sent a professional email without writing any code!
